A featured escape

Ananthagiri Hills, Vikarabad

Just two hours from Hyderabad, Ananthagiri is our go-to for a first-time overnight in the wild. Pitch tents by the hillside, kayak in the morning, and end the night around a fire under an open sky.

1

4:00 PM · Arrive & settle in

Tent allocation, welcome drink, outdoor and indoor games as the group finds its rhythm.

2

Evening · Tea, music & bonfire

Tea and snacks, campfire, music, and swimming or rain-dancing on select packages.

3

9:30 PM · Dinner under the stars

A hearty vegetarian or non-vegetarian dinner served in the open air.

4

9:00 AM · Breakfast · 11:00 AM · Check-out

Buffet breakfast, one last walk around the hills and an unhurried check-out.

From ₹1,999 per person · group discounts available · AC rooms, triangle dorms & camping tents · meals, tea & snacks included · Aadhaar card mandatory · transport not included.
