Our story

The beginning of Cliff-Inn Adventures

It all started with four adventure enthusiasts, gathered around a cafe, sharing a common dream - to make adventure accessible to everyone. With a passion for exploration and a vision to create meaningful outdoor experiences, they set out to build something extraordinary. This dream gave birth to Cliff-Inn Adventures - a dedicated team of skilled professionals and nature enthusiasts, united by a love for adventure and a commitment to sharing it with the world.

At the heart of our journey lies a deep focus on safety, personal growth, and the power of outdoor experiences. We go beyond just activities; we foster connections, self-discovery, and the excitement of pushing boundaries. At Cliff-Inn Adventures, we believe that adventure is a universal language - one that transcends age, background, and experience. Our mission is to create unforgettable moments that inspire, challenge, and leave lasting memories.

How we operate

Every experience is by design

At Cliff-Inn Adventures, every experience is designed to inspire, challenge, and create lasting memories. We believe that adventure is more than just an activity - it's a journey of discovery, learning, and growth.

With a passion for outdoor exploration and experiential learning, we craft unique and immersive experiences for schools and corporations. Questions & answers

Everything you need to know

We design experiences for ages 8 and up. School programs are grouped by grade, corporate outings are matched to team fitness levels, and independent trips list a suggested age range so you can pick the right adventure.

Safety is built into every trip. We use certified gear, brief every participant before an activity, maintain small instructor-to-participant ratios, and carry a written safety protocol plus first-aid support on every outing.

After booking you receive a detailed packing list. Generally: comfortable trekking clothes, a reusable water bottle, sturdy shoes, a light jacket, and personal medication. We provide tents, sleeping gear, safety equipment, and meals.

Yes. Overnight camps include tents, sleeping mats, and all meals. Day outings include snacks and refreshments. Dietary preferences can be shared at booking and we accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and common allergies.

Click “Plan Your Escape” or “Register Interest” on any page, share your group size and preferred dates, and our team will call back within 24 hours with a tailored plan. No payment is required to reserve your interest.

Cancellations made 14 days or more before departure receive a full refund. Between 7–14 days, 50% is refunded. Within 7 days, the trip is non-refundable but transferable to another participant or a future date subject to availability.
